Three steps after install

1
Make it your assistant
Settings → Apps → Default apps → Digital assistant app → Star Storyteller. The assist gesture and the power-button hold then open the chat.
2
Pick a model
Tap the model chip at the top of the chat and choose one. It downloads in place; Gemma-4-E2B is a good first choice on most phones.
3
Connect your search
Tap the Web chip and point it at your SearXNG instance. It needs json under search.formats — the Test connection button checks that for you.
